1. This policy outlines the core employee benefits provided by the company.
2. Full-time regular employees working 30+ hours weekly are eligible.
3. Coverage for health insurance begins on the first day of employment.
4. The company pays a major share of standard medical plan premiums.
5. Dental and vision options are available during open enrollment periods.
6. Paid time off (PTO) accrues every pay period for full-time staff.
7. Employees receive ten paid national holidays designated each year.
8. Sick leave is provided separately to handle personal health needs.
9. The 401(k) retirement plan includes an employer match up to four percent.
10. Employees vest immediately in all personal and matching retirement funds.
11. Life insurance equal to one year's salary is provided at no cost.
12. Short-term and long-term disability coverage protects income during illness.
13. The employee assistance program offers free and confidential counseling.
14. Tuition reimbursement covers up to two thousand dollars per calendar year.
15. Parental leave grants up to twelve weeks of paid time for new parents.
16. Flexible working hours help staff balance personal and professional duties.
17. Annual wellness stipends support gym memberships and healthy habits.
18. Benefit elections remain locked unless a qualifying life event occurs.
19. Qualifying events include marriage, birth, or loss of other coverage.
20. Human resources handles all questions regarding claims and plan rules.
