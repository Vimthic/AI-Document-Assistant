1. This policy sets the core rules for company spending and money management. 
2. All staff must follow these rules when they use company funds. 
3. The finance team must check and approve all budgets before spending starts. 
4. Workers must get approval from a boss before they buy items for work. 
5. You must keep every receipt for any business cost you pay. 
6. Submit your money back forms within thirty days of the cost date. 
7. The firm does not pay back money for personal items or food. 
8. Travel costs must use the cheapest safe choice available. 
9. Plane tickets need a booking two weeks before the trip date. 
10. Hotel costs have a set limit for each night of stay. 
11. Client meals need a list of all people who attend the meal. 
12. The company pays for work car use by a set rate per mile. 
13. Large asset buys need two price quotes from different sellers. 
14. No worker can sign a money contract over five thousand dollars alone. 
15. The firm pays all vendor bills within thirty days of getting them. 
16. The cash drawer must balance at the end of each work day. 
17. Audits of all financial records will happen twice every year. 
18. Any lost receipt means the worker might not get their money back. 
19. Fraud or bad use of company funds leads to job loss at once. 
20. Ask the finance head if you have questions about this policy.
